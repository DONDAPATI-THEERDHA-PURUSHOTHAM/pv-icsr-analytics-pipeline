Enterprise Pharmacovigilance and Safety Governance Policy
Document Reference: POL-2026-PV-001
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes mandatory procedures for Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR) processing, 15-day expedited reporting to regulatory authorities (FDA 21 CFR 314.80), and periodic safety review.

2. Risk Classification and Signal Detection
All post-marketing adverse experience reports must undergo statistical disproportionality evaluation using Proportional Reporting Ratios (PRR). Any signal with a PRR score exceeding 2.0 and minimum 3 case occurrences requires escalation to the Safety Committee.

3. Cryptographic Audit Controls
In compliance with 21 CFR Part 11, all safety summary reports and generated payload metrics must be cryptographically hashed using SHA-256 prior to final authorization.
